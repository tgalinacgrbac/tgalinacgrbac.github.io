Call for Participation

Towards Cross-Domain Evidence, Comparative Studies, and Systematic Adoption Frameworks

The workshop continues the research alignment initiative started at MIPRO 2026 and focuses on building cumulative, comparable evidence on LLM adoption across domains.

Workshop Objectives

  • Present and discuss new cross-domain experiences with LLM adoption.
  • Further refine the analytical framework introduced at MIPRO.
  • Identify recurring human-centered and socio-technical patterns.
  • Expand the empirical dataset through additional case studies.
  • Strengthen an international research community around systematic LLM adoption.

How to Participate

  • Join as a discussion participant or presenter.
  • Optional short presentation (5-10 minutes), with advance notice to organizers.
  • Submit your contribution through EDAS using the workshop track link above.

Framework Discussion Questions

A. Problem - What problem is being solved?
B. Solution - How is it solved?
C. Human Interaction - How do humans engage?
D. Context - Under what conditions?
E. Evaluation - What is the effect?
F. Challenges - What limits it?
G. Benefits - What value is created?
H. Transferability - What generalizes?
I. Process - How can successful adoption be reproduced?
