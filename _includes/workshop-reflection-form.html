SOFTCOM 2026 – Human-Centered LLM Adoption Reflection Sheet

This questionnaire is part of the Research Alignment Workshop II: Human-Centered Adoption of Large Language Models.

Its purpose is twofold: to reflect on LLM adoption cases using a common set of analytical questions, and to critically examine whether the proposed analytical framework adequately captures human-centered LLM adoption across different domains.

Please reflect either on your own LLM application/case or on one of the case studies presented during the workshop. There are no correct or incorrect answers. Short responses are sufficient.

Section 2 — Apply the analytical framework

Short responses are sufficient. Please answer each question in relation to the selected case.

What problem, need, or limitation motivates the LLM application? Briefly describe how the LLM is used and how it is integrated into the broader system or workflow. Who interacts with or is affected by the system? What role do humans have in using, supervising, interpreting, or controlling it? Which organizational, technical, operational, regulatory, or other contextual conditions influence the application? How is success or impact evaluated?
