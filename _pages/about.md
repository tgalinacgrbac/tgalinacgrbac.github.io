Tihana Galinac Grbac is a Full Professor of Computer Science at Juraj Dobrila University of Pula, Croatia, and head of the Software Engineering and Information Processing Laboratory (SEIP Lab). She previously worked in industry at Ericsson Nikola Tesla, where she was involved in large-scale core network software development and software process improvement initiatives.

She received a Ph.D. (2009) and M.Sc. (2005) from the University of Zagreb, Croatia. Her research and professional work connect software engineering theory with empirical and industrial practice.

## Research Interests

- Software and systems engineering
- Large-scale, complex, and distributed software systems
- Software evolution and product lines
- Sustainability, reliability, safety, and performance
- Trustworthy and responsible computing
- Software-defined networks and Internet of Services
- Software process improvement and software engineering management
- Applied statistics, AI, soft computing, and search-based optimization
- Experimentation in software engineering with industrial focus
- Digital and smart systems (smart cities, smart government, smart industries)

## Current Roles

- Full Professor, Juraj Dobrila University of Pula (2018-present)
- Head of SEIP Lab, Juraj Dobrila University of Pula (2018-present)
- Scientific Advisor (highest scientific degree in Croatia)
