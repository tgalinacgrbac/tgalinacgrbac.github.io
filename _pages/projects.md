## Selected Research Projects

- **[EQUISYS (2025-2029)](/equisys/):** Human-Centered System Design for Equitable and Sustainable Digital Societies. Scientific research project led by Tihana Galinac Grbac at the Juraj Dobrila University of Pula.
- **[Water4Reuse (2024-2028)](https://www.cost.eu/actions/CA23104):** Mainstreaming water reuse into the circular economy paradigm. EU COST Action CA23104.
- **[CERCIRAS (2020-2024)](https://www.cerciras.org/):** Connecting Education and Research Communities for an Innovative Resource-Aware Society. EU COST Action CA19135.
- **[RELYSOFT (2020-2024)](https://www.unipu.hr/seiplab/istrazivanje/relysoft):** Reliable and Safe Complex Software Systems: from Empirical Principles towards Theoretical Models in View of Industrial Applications. Principal Investigator, Croatian Science Foundation.
- **[SusTrainable (2020-2023)](https://www.unipu.hr/en/international-cooperation/projects/sustrainable):** Promoting Sustainability as a Fundamental Driver in Software Development Training and Education. Croatian Principal Investigator, Erasmus+.
- **[EVOSOFT (2015-2018)](https://www.seiplab.riteh.uniri.hr/?page_id=712&lang=en):** Evolving Software Systems: Analysis and Innovative Approaches for Smart Management ([UNIPU project page](https://fet.unipu.hr/seiplab/en/research/evosoft)). Principal Investigator, Croatian Science Foundation.
- **Doctoral Student Funded within the project Evolving Software Systems: Analysis and Innovative Approaches for Smart Management (EVOSOFT) (2015-2018):** HRZZ-DOK-2015-10. Principal Investigator, Croatian Science Foundation doctoral student project. Project leader: Prof. Tihana Galinac Grbac.
- **3COWS (2018-2019):** Focusing Education on Composability, Comprehensibility and Correctness of Working Software. Croatian Principal Investigator, Erasmus+.
- **An Empirical Comparison of Machine Learning Based Approaches/Techniques for Code Smell Detection (2018-2019):** Croatian Principal Investigator, bilateral Croatian-Slovenian project. Project leader for Croatia: Prof. Tihana Galinac Grbac.
- **Analysis and Innovative Approaches to Development, Management and Application of Complex Software Systems (2013-2018):** Principal Investigator, University of Rijeka research grant project.
- **ACROSS (2013-2017):** Autonomous Control for a Reliable Internet of Services. Croatian Management Committee Member, EU COST Action 1304.
- **BETTY (2012-2016):** Behavioural Types for Large-Scale Reliable Systems. Croatian Management Committee Member, EU COST Action 1201.
- **International Cooperation in Computer Science (2014-present):** Steering Committee member and local coordinator, CEEPUS network.
- **New Architectures and Protocols in Converged Telecommunication Networks (2007-2009):** Croatian Ministry of Science, Education, and Sport-funded research project. Project leader: Prof. Darko Huljenić.
- **Optimisation and Design of Time-Frequency Distributions (2007-2009):** Croatian Ministry of Science, Education, and Sport-funded research project. Project leader: Prof. Viktor Sučić.
